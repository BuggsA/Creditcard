Credits and perks

  • $100 hotel credit — On hotel stays booked through Chase Travel. We count about $75 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • Global Entry / TSA PreCheck credit
  • Purchase protection
  • Foreign transaction fee: none

Who it's for

  • A first travel card that's easy to get value from
  • Diners who also want gas and streaming rewards
  • Not ideal: People who'd never book travel through Chase

Can I get it?

  • Recommended credit: Good
  • Chase rules: 5/24: usually denied if you've opened 5+ personal cards (any bank) in the last 24 months. The bonus may be unavailable if you've had this card before.
  • Visa: Accepted almost everywhere, including Costco warehouses (Visa only).
