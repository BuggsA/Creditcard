American Express Platinum Card: what it's really worth

The American Express Platinum Card has a $895 annual fee. For a typical household spending $2,070 a month, it's worth about $1,720 in year one and $520 each year after.

Is the annual fee worth it?

Worth keeping after year one for typical spending: it nets about $23 a year more than the Wells Fargo Active Cash, the best no-fee card for the same spending.

  • $300 digital entertainment credit — $25/month on select streaming & news subscriptions. We count about $150 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • $200 airline fee credit — Incidental fees on one airline you choose. We count about $100 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • $200 Uber Cash — $15/month plus $20 in December. We count about $120 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.

Who it's for

  • Lounge lovers — the widest airport lounge access
  • People who'll actually use many monthly credits
  • Not ideal: Everyday spending — only 1x outside flights
  • Not ideal: Anyone who doesn't want to track credits

Can I get it?

  • Recommended credit: Excellent
  • American Express rules: Welcome offers are generally once per lifetime per card. Amex usually tells you before approval if you won't get the bonus.
  • Amex: Not accepted everywhere: less common abroad and at some small businesses. Not accepted at Costco warehouses.
