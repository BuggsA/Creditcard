Delta SkyMiles Gold: what it's really worth

The Delta SkyMiles Gold has a $150 annual fee ($0 the first year). For a typical household spending $2,070 a month, it's worth about $1,322 in year one and $292 each year after.

Is the annual fee worth it?

Probably not worth the fee after year one for typical spending: the no-fee Hilton Honors Card would net about $214 a year more. It can still make sense if you spend more in its bonus categories or use more of its credits.
