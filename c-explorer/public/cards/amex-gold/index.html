American Express Gold Card: what it's really worth

The American Express Gold Card has a $325 annual fee. For a typical household spending $2,070 a month, it's worth about $1,701 in year one and $801 each year after.

Credits and perks

  • $120 dining credit — $10/month at participating partners. We count about $80 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • $120 Uber Cash — $10/month for Uber rides or Uber Eats. We count about $90 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • $100 Resy credit — $50 twice a year at U.S. Resy restaurants. We count about $75 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • $84 Dunkin' credit — $7/month at U.S. Dunkin'. We count about $40 a year, since most people don't use every dollar.
  • Purchase protection
  • Foreign transaction fee: none

Who it's for

  • Big grocery and restaurant spenders
  • Foodies who'll use the monthly dining credits
  • Not ideal: People who won't use monthly credits — the fee is $325

Can I get it?

  • Recommended credit: Good
  • American Express rules: Welcome offers are generally once per lifetime per card. Amex usually tells you before approval if you won't get the bonus.
  • Amex: Not accepted everywhere: less common abroad and at some small businesses. Not accepted at Costco warehouses.
