Privacy

CardCove has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. The spending you enter stays in your browser.

What's stored, and where

  • Your settings (spending, filters, cards you have) are saved in your own browser's local storage so they're there next time, and in the page address so a shared link shows the same table. They're never sent to us.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers run on this site.
  • Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Issuer links take you to the card issuer's site, where their privacy policy applies.

To clear your saved settings, clear this site's data in your browser.
