About CardCompass

How does CardCompass work out what a credit card is worth?

It adds up the card's sign-up bonus, the rewards it earns on the monthly spending you enter (respecting category caps), and a realistic share of its credits, then subtracts the annual fee. Points are valued at our estimates, and you can switch to cash-out (about 1¢) or transfer-partner (about 2¢) values.

Is an annual-fee card worth it after the first year?

The Yr 2+ column shows each card's value every year after the bonus. A ▲ means the card beats the best no-fee card for your spending, and each card's row explains how much of its credits you'd need to use to break even.

Which card should I get next if I already have some?

Add the cards you already have under “Cards I already have”. CardCompass then ranks each new card by what it adds on top of your wallet, and flags cards you're unlikely to be approved for, such as Chase's 5/24 rule.

Does CardCompass check my credit or cost anything?

No. CardCompass is free, runs in your browser and never checks your credit. Offers change often and many vary by applicant, so confirm the terms with the issuer before you apply.
